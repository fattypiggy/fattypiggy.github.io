About Publications

William Jing

I am a Ph.D. student in Information Science at the University of North Texas. My research focuses on diffusion-based image generation, portrait relighting, and illumination estimation. I am particularly interested in controllable image generation that preserves visual fidelity and scene content.

I received my M.S. in Computer Science from Fudan University and my B.S. in Computer Science from Donghua University. Previously, I worked as an Applied Scientist Intern at Amazon and a Machine Learning Research Intern at OPPO Research Institute.
